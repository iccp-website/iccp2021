Hybrid Program:
The conference was held in hybrid format (physical attendance WITH online streaming and participation). On May/23, we held the event physically in the Meridian Hotel in Haifa. The talks were also be streamed for virtual attendance. On May/24+25, the conference was held purely in remote (virtual) mode.

Note

There is an unrelated website which claims that our 2021 International Conference on Computational Photography will be held at a location away from Haifa, in another country, and not in May.  We apologize, but we have no control of whoever creates arbitrary content online.
